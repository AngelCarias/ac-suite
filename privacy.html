AC Suite Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

AC Suite is a private tool used only by its owner. It is not distributed to other users.

Google user data

When the owner signs in with Google, AC Suite accesses Google Drive, Google Calendar and Google Tasks data from the owner's own account, only to show and sync that data on the owner's own computers.

  • Data is stored only on the owner's computers and in the owner's own Google account.
  • No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or analytics.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

AC Suite's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
